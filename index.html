PhilaLink

The Story Behind PhilaLink

PhilaLink began with something I have witnessed for most of my life.

For more than 15 years, I have lived with my uncle's wife, who still serves as a medication proxy in our community. Through her, I have seen the dedication it takes to collect chronic medication for elderly and vulnerable patients, as well as the challenges that come with the current paper-based proxy system. Lost collection cards, forgotten collection dates, long queues, and communication gaps between patients and clinics are problems that affect people every day.

Watching these challenges firsthand made me realise that technology could simplify the process and improve the experience for everyone involved. That idea became the foundation of PhilaLink.

PhilaLink is a digital healthcare platform designed to modernise the proxy medication collection process within South Africa's public healthcare system. It replaces paper-based processes with a secure digital platform that helps caregivers manage multiple patients, keeps patients informed through automated medication reminders, and provides tools that make healthcare more accessible. The platform also includes clinic location services and intelligent health support to assist users in making informed decisions about their wellbeing.

One of the platform's key features is Phila Chat, an AI-powered health assistant that allows users to describe their symptoms and declare any known allergies before receiving personalised health guidance. Based on the information provided, Phila Chat recommends suitable over-the-counter medication where appropriate, offers self-care advice for minor conditions, and encourages users to seek professional medical attention when symptoms require further assessment.

The name PhilaLink reflects the purpose of the platform. It combines the Nguni word Phila, meaning "to be healthy" or "to live", with the English word Link, representing connection. Together, the name symbolises a connection between patients, caregivers, healthcare professionals, and the services that support healthier communities.

PhilaLink is more than a final-year project to me. It is a solution inspired by someone close to me who continues to serve our community as a medication proxy. It represents my belief that software can solve real problems, strengthen public healthcare, and improve people's everyday lives.
